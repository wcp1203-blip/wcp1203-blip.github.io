## Academic Research

### Multi-Authority Identity-Based Encryption (IBE)
* **Description**: Developing a multi-KGC (Key Generation Center) Identity-Based Encryption scheme to address the key escrow problem inherent in traditional IBE systems. 
* **Focus**: Applied cryptography, threshold cryptography, and provable security.
